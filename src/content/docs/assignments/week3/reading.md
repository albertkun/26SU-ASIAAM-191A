The readings this week are designed to show development (and lack) of ethics from the past and to the present. Jeremy Crampton's paper shows a past view of GIS and ethics in 1995, while Paris Marx's article describes the lack of a systematic approach to view social problems with technology in "Don't Blame Social Media. Blame Capitalism."

- [Crampton, J. (1995) The Ethics of GIS.](../../../readings/CramptonCaGISEthics1995.pdf) [10-15 minutes]
- [Marx, P. (2020) Don't Blame Social Media. Blame Capitalism.](https://www.jacobinmag.com/2020/09/social-media-platform-capitalism-the-social-dilemma) [10-15 minutes]
